How pricing works
  1. Total USD = Price + Shipping + Duty + Transport + Charge + Clearance
  2. Landed Cost (Local) = Total USD × Exchange Rate
  3. Selling Price = Landed Cost × (1 + Margin %)
  4. Part Number = Auto S/N + space + Manual Item Number (e.g. SN-0001 BRAKE-001)
  5. On sale, stock is deducted and an invoice is auto-generated.
